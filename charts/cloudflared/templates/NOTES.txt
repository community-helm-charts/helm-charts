Cloudflared connectors have been deployed in remotely managed mode.

Check the rollout:
  kubectl rollout status deployment/{{ include "cloudflared.fullname" . }} --namespace {{ include "common.names.namespace" . }}

List connector pods:
  kubectl get pods --namespace {{ include "common.names.namespace" . }} \
    -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }},app.kubernetes.io/component=connector

View connector logs:
  kubectl logs --namespace {{ include "common.names.namespace" . }} \
    deployment/{{ include "cloudflared.fullname" . }} --all-containers --prefix

Configure tunnel routes remotely in Cloudflare. A cluster Service can be reached
with an address such as http://service.namespace.svc.{{ .Values.clusterDomain }}:port.
